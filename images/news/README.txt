Any event without a photo just shows the headline.

  nvidia-5-trillion.jpg     Nvidia worth $5 trillion

Tips
- Wide photos work best here (e.g. 1200 x 675).
- Using .png? Change the ending on that event's "photo" line in js/news-events.js.
- Add a new event by copying one in js/news-events.js and giving it a new photo name.
